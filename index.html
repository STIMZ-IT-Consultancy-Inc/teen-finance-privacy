Teen Finance Tracker

Privacy Policy

STIMZ IT Consultancy Inc.

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Teen Finance Tracker ("the app") is made by STIMZ IT Consultancy Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. Contact: privacy@stimzit.com

Summary

  • We only collect what the app needs to work: your email, your password (stored securely), and the money information you type in.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Only you can see your data. You can delete your account and all of your data in the app at any time.

Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and password. Passwords are stored by our sign-in provider in a protected (hashed) form; we can never see your password.

Information you enter. Transactions (type, amount, category, description, date), custom categories, monthly budgets, savings goals, repeating transactions, and your spending-warning settings.

Two-step verification (optional). If you turn it on, our sign-in provider stores the secret key that your authenticator app uses, so it can check your 6-digit codes.

Technical information. When the app talks to our servers, our providers keep short-term server logs (such as IP address, time of the request and error details) for security and troubleshooting.

Stored only on your phone. Your colour theme and daily reminder time. Reminders are notifications created on your phone, not sent from our servers. When you export your transactions, the file is made on your phone and shared only where you choose.

What we don't collect. Your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising ID or other device identifiers. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

How we use your information

Only to run the app: to create your account and sign you in, to save and show your own transactions, categories, budgets, goals and settings, to keep your account secure, and to send account emails (for example a password reset link). We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Who we share it with

Only with the service provider that runs the app for us:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and account emails). Your data is stored on Supabase servers in the United States (West US, Oregon).

We may also share information if the law requires it. We never share it for marketing.

Who can see your data

Only you. Each account can only read and change its own data; this is enforced by our database's security rules, not just by the app. If you turn on two-step verification, your data can't be opened until the 6-digit code from your authenticator app has been entered.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. You can add two-step verification in Settings → Security. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children and teens

The app is intended for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at privacy@stimzit.com and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. You can edit or delete any transaction, category, budget or goal in the app at any time.

Delete your account in the app: go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all of your transactions, categories, budgets, goals, repeating transactions and settings straight away.

Delete your account without the app: email privacy@stimzit.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will delete your account and its data within 30 days. Short-term server logs are removed automatically by our provider.

Your rights

You can see, correct and delete your information in the app, and you can ask us for a copy of it or ask questions about how it is handled by emailing privacy@stimzit.com. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under privacy laws, and you can contact your local privacy regulator if you have concerns.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
